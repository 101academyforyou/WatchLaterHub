WatchLaterHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

WatchLaterHub (“the extension”) is a new tab extension for Chrome. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, how it is used, and what it never does.

1. Data the extension accesses

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Google account name, email and profile pictureShow which account is currently signed inOnly in your browser (chrome.storage)
Google OAuth access tokenCall the YouTube Data APIOnly in your browser
YouTube playlists (youtube.readonly)Show a random saved video on the new tab pageOnly in your browser
YouTube Watch later list (only when you select it)The YouTube API does not provide this list, so the extension uses the YouTube session you are already signed in to in this browser to read your own Watch later page on youtube.com. To load lists longer than 100 videos, it reads the youtube.com sign-in cookie (SAPISID) to build the authorization header YouTube requiresThe list is stored only in your browser; the cookie is never stored and is sent only back to youtube.com
Videos you add manually, to-dos, notes and attachments (images, PDFs and other files), timers, settingsProvide the saved videos, to-do, notes, reminder and timer featuresOnly in your browser
Chrome bookmarksShow, add, edit and reorder bookmarksManaged by Chrome; the extension keeps no separate copy
Chrome history (recent URLs and titles)List recently visited pages in the “Recent” panelOnly in your browser
Location (only when you click “Use current location”)Look up local weatherOnly in your browser
Current tab URL (only when you click the WatchLaterHub icon in the toolbar)Check whether the current tab is a new tab page, to switch between WatchLaterHub and Chrome’s default new tab pageNot stored

2. Where data is sent

The extension has no server of its own and never sends your data to the developer. Only to provide its features does your browser connect directly to these services:

  • Google (YouTube Data API, Google OAuth, youtube.com): sign-in, and read-only access (youtube.readonly) to your playlists and Watch later list. The extension never modifies anything in your YouTube account.
  • Open-Meteo, BigDataCloud, OpenStreetMap Nominatim: weather and place-name lookups. Only coordinates or the city name you type are sent, with no identifying information.
  • YouTube thumbnails, Google favicon service: show video thumbnails and site icons.

3. What we never do

  • Sell, rent or transfer your data to any third party
  • Use your data for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to the extension
  • Track your browsing or run analytics
  • Access your Gmail or data from other Google services (only read-only YouTube access is used)

The extension’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Deleting data and revoking access

  • Click “Sign out” in the “Saved” window to revoke Google access and delete the token
  • Removing the extension deletes all the data it stored in your browser
  • You can also revoke access at any time on your Google Account permissions page
