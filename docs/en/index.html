A small new tab with just the right features

Nothing more, nothing less. Ready the moment you open it.

One saved video in every tab

A random pick from your saved videos, never the same one twice in a row. Not in the mood for this one? Just click “Shuffle”.

Sign in with Google, sync automatically

Click Continue with Google and choose which lists to sync: Watch later or your own playlists. They sync right away and refresh automatically every time you open “Saved”.

Bring in a whole playlist at once

Paste a playlist URL and click “Extract links”. Every video link in the playlist is lined up for you to copy, or add to your saved videos in one click.

Save great videos without worrying

WatchLaterHub helps you rediscover the great videos you always meant to watch but forgot about.

Search just like before

Start typing in a new tab to search. It uses the default search engine you set in Chrome and never changes your search settings.

Your data stays with you

Your saved list lives only in your own browser. Signing in asks for read-only YouTube access — it never likes, comments, or changes any of your lists.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No, it’s completely free.

Do I have to sign in with Google?

No. You can paste video URLs by hand, or import a whole playlist from its URL.

What if I don’t want to see a certain video?

Click the ✕ next to the video in “Manage saved” to remove it. You can also uncheck an entire playlist so no more videos are picked from it.

About WatchLaterHub

A free Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with a random video from your YouTube Watch later list and playlists, plus your bookmarks and a to-do list. Its search box uses the default search engine you chose in Chrome. When you sign in with Google, it requests read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly) solely to load your playlists. Your data is stored only in your browser; it is never sold, shared, or sent to our servers. Privacy Policy
